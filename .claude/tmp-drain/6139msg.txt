fix(delivery): the per-Organization console pin had no writer for 3.5 months

`images.console`, `images.admin` and `images.marketplaceApi` were frozen at
`3c2f7e4` (2026-04-28) for three and a half months. Their build workflows
sed-ed the `image:` line of the chart template; PR #580 (83ec889f0,
2026-05-02) turned those lines into Helm expressions and moved the mutable
SHA into values.yaml, so the seds matched zero lines, exited 0, and every
run reported success against an empty diff.

Adds `scripts/bump-values-image-tag.sh` (fails closed if the pin field is
reshaped, asserts the write landed), `scripts/check-image-pin-writer-targets.sh`
+ its test matrix, and extends `check-controller-image-tag-freshness.sh` to
cover these three pins. console-build / admin-build / marketplace-api-build
now write through the new writer.

Rebased onto main and flattened; chart re-serialized to 1.4.1382 through
`scripts/bump-chart-version.sh` (main baseline 1.4.1379, skipping the
1.4.1380/1.4.1381 claims held by open PRs). `images.catalystUi.tag` keeps
main's deploy-bot value.
