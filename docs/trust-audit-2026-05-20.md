The single pattern across all 3 theater closures: **the bulk-template "not-on-pillar-path" comment was used to close issues that had concrete still-failing evidence in their own comment threads, or that the issue body explicitly said should stay OPEN until a fresh-prov walk verified.**

## Anti-patterns confirmed in this audit

1. **Bulk-template closure overriding live evidence**: #1741 had a still-failing walk-report 2 hours before closure; #1882 had a first-comment explicitly saying "keeping open"; both got closed with the same canned template anyway.
2. **Stable-state walk passed off as fresh-prov walk**: #1819 closed citing a t22 treemap-renders observation, when the issue body explicitly required a NEXT-fresh-prov zero-touch walk.
3. **Admin-merge through red CI**: PR #1779 (closed #1745) merged with both `pin-sync-audit` and `manifest-validation` FAILURE — §4 rule 5 violation.
