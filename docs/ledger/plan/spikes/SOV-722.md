# SOV-722 Spike: the k3s release that passes both registry-drain cases shared and silent-peer (result)

Outcome: fail as shipped. No k3s release passes both cases with the harness exactly as it is on
`hold/SOV-721` (a3d54858a7). k3s v1.33.13+k3s2 passes both once the silent-peer witness counts
rd-agent-2's data packets to the silent port, not only the SYN of a new connection. That witness is
in `scripts/check-registry-drain.sh`, which is a path of SOV-721.

## Question

SOV-721 moved the kit from k3s v1.31.8+k3s1 to v1.31.14+k3s1. The goal was a node mirror that gives
up on a peer that accepts a connection and never answers. The corrected harness of SOV-721 then
showed a regression. Case `shared` fails 4 of 4 in CI on v1.31.14+k3s1 and is green on main with
v1.31.8+k3s1. The plan keeps the kit on v1.31.8+k3s1 until one k3s release passes both:

- `shared`: the registry's only copy and two Pods of a test image run on rd-agent-1, the only node
  that holds their images. rd-agent-1 is drained, stopped for 60 s and started. Within 420 s of its
  return, every Pod runs and is Ready, and no container waits on an image. The peers must receive the
  images that were imported on one node.
- `silent-peer`: rd-agent-1's registry port sends its SYN-ACK and nothing after it. rd-agent-2's pull
  of the probe must finish from the registry within 60 s. The pull must have asked the silent peer,
  and the registry must have served the probe's manifest.

The search order: the later patches of the 1.31 line, then 1.32, then 1.33.

## What was run

- Host: one Docker host, kernel 5.15.0-76-generic, Docker 29.1.3, 8 CPUs, 15 GiB. Containers only:
  no cluster and no kubeconfig (`KUBECONFIG=/dev/null`). This host's Docker socket needs root, so
  `docker` was a wrapper that runs `sudo -n docker` and gives the files of `docker save` back to the
  calling user.
- Harness: `scripts/check-registry-drain.sh` at a3d54858a7 (`hold/SOV-721`), run with
  `REGISTRY_DRAIN_ANY_HOST=1` and `NO_COLOR=1`, with yq v4.44.3 first in `PATH`.
- Choosing the release: the harness reads the default of `k3s_version` in
  `infra/providers/local/variables.tf`, and the image list
  `infra/providers/_shared/k3s-images/<release>.txt`. In a scratch checkout of a3d54858a7, the
  runner set that default to the candidate and added the release's published `k3s-images.txt`.
  Nothing else was changed. The runner restores `variables.tf` at the end.
- Release facts were read from the code host's raw files at each tag: the `spegel` replace line of
  `go.mod`, `pkg/agent/containerd/containerd.go` of k3s, and `pkg/registry/registry.go` and
  `pkg/oci/containerd.go` of the k3s fork of spegel.
- The experiment stays on branch `spike/SOV-722`, which is never merged. It is based on a3d54858a7.
  - `tests/spikes/SOV-722/run.sh` is the runner. `tests/spikes/SOV-722/bin/docker` is the wrapper.
  - `tests/spikes/SOV-722/runs.tsv` and `tests/spikes/SOV-722/logs/` hold every run.
  - `scripts/check-registry-drain-datapackets.sh` and `scripts/check-registry-drain-anysource.sh` are
    the two witness variants below.
  - The image lists of the candidates are under `infra/providers/_shared/k3s-images/`.

Two witness variants were used for silent-peer only, after the harness as shipped failed v1.33.13+k3s2
on its witness. Each changes the rule that counts what rd-agent-2 asks of the silent port, plus the
words of three messages. Nothing else changes.

```
# check-registry-drain-datapackets.sh, line 787 (was: ... --dport "$port" --syn \)
in_net "$pid" iptables -w 10 -t raw -I PREROUTING 1 -p tcp -s "$ip2" --dport "$port" -m length --length 100:65535 \
# check-registry-drain-anysource.sh, line 787: any source except loopback
in_net "$pid" iptables -w 10 -t raw -I PREROUTING 1 ! -i lo -p tcp --dport "$port" -m length --length 100:65535 \
```

A packet of 100 bytes or more on that port carries data: a TLS record of a request, or a ClientHello.
A bare ACK or a TCP keep-alive (with timestamps) is 52 or 53 bytes, and a TLS alert stays under 100.

## The releases after v1.31.14+k3s1

The 1.31 line has no later patch: v1.31.14+k3s1 is its last release (`git ls-remote --tags` of
k3s-io/k3s, 25 November 2026). Two properties decide the cases:

- Labels on imported content. spegel v0.4.0 and later advertise content only when it has a
  `containerd.io/distribution.source.<registry>` label. Content that k3s imports from a tarball had
  none until k3s added `labelContent` (`pkg/agent/containerd/containerd.go`, commented "required for
  spegel to properly filter content from images that are imported"). It first ships in v1.32.11+k3s1
  and v1.33.7+k3s1. v1.31.14+k3s1, v1.32.10+k3s1 and v1.33.6+k3s1 do not have it.
- A bound on every request to a peer:
  - v0.0.23, v0.0.30 and v0.2.0 bound only the peer lookup: one `context.WithTimeout`, on
    `resolveTimeout`.
  - v0.4.0-k3s3 sends HEAD to the peer with a 1 s limit, GET of a manifest with 2 s, and HEAD with 1 s
    before every blob (`pkg/registry/registry.go` lines 337, 351 and 372). Its `registry.go` is
    byte for byte the one of v0.4.0-k3s3.32.
  - v0.6.0-k3s1 and -k3s2 bound HEAD (1 s) and GET of a manifest (2 s), not GET of a blob (lines 306
    and 310).
  - v0.7.0-k3s1 and v0.7.1-k3s1 bound HEAD and manifests to 3 s (line 258), not a blob.
  - v0.7.2-k3s1 also races every in-flight fetch against `max(2 x the hedger's highest percentile,
    100 ms)` (lines 375 to 399), which covers blobs.

### shared

- Labelled imports are necessary but not enough. Without `labelContent`, v1.32.10+k3s1 (run 13)
  fails exactly as v1.31.14+k3s1 failed for SOV-721: rd-agent-2 cannot take busybox from rd-agent-1.
  v1.32.11+k3s1 (run 3) has the same spegel v0.4.0-k3s3.32 with `labelContent`, and it fails the
  same way. This matches SOV-721's measurement that v0.4.0 finds a labelled image by digest but not
  by tag.
- spegel v0.6.0 and v0.7.2 together with `labelContent` pass. v1.32.13+k3s1 passed once (run 1).
  v1.33.13+k3s2 passed twice (runs 5 and 12) and recovered within 1 s of rd-agent-1's return each
  time.

### silent-peer

- v1.32.9+k3s1 (spegel v0.2.0, run 14) hangs as v1.31.8+k3s1 does. rd-agent-2 opened 1 connection to
  the silent port, the pull did not finish within 60 s, and the registry was never asked.
- v1.32.13+k3s1 (spegel v0.6.0) hangs in all three runs (2, 10 and 15). The registry log shows the
  manifest HEAD at 10:37:15.6 and the manifest GET at 10:37:17.6 (run 2). The 2.0 s between them is
  the fork's limit on a manifest GET to the peer. No blob request reached the registry: spegel's GET
  of a blob from the silent peer has no limit. Under the any-source witness, 3 data packets went to the silent
  port (run 15). Under the data witness, which counts only rd-agent-2's node address, the count is 0
  (run 10). So this fork reaches the peer from another source address; its verdict rests on the time
  limit in either form.
- v1.32.11+k3s1 (spegel v0.4.0, run 4) did not reach the silent step. Its witness, rd-agent-2 taking
  the probe that rd-agent-1 had pulled, failed within 90 s. SOV-721 measured the same on this host for
  v1.31.14+k3s1 (same fork), and recorded that the step passed 4 of 4 times on GitHub's runner. That
  release fails `shared` either way.
- v1.33.13+k3s2 (spegel v0.7.2) finishes the pull in 1 s in all three measured runs (6, 9 and 11). The
  registry served the probe's manifest HEAD and GET and both blobs, all in the same second (run 6,
  10:54:22).

### Why the shipped witness fails v1.33.13+k3s2

The shipped witness counts SYNs from rd-agent-2 to the silent port (`--syn`, line 787). spegel
v0.7.2's transport keeps idle connections to a peer for 90 s (`pkg/httpx/httpx.go` line 28,
`IdleConnTimeout`; 100 per host, `pkg/oci/client.go` line 69). The case's own witness pull from
rd-agent-1 takes place 28 s before the measured pull (run 6: 10:53:54 and 10:54:22). So the measured
pull's request to the peer travels on that kept connection and sends no SYN. The data witness counts
it: 2 and 3 data packets from rd-agent-2's node address during the pull (runs 9 and 11), while the
silent port withheld 12 and 9 packets.

### The data witness can fail

Under the data witness, v1.32.13+k3s1 still fails on the time limit (run 10). Both variants keep
every other judgement of the case: the TLS judge (connection accepted, no handshake within 10 s), the
pull judge (60 s), at least 1 counted request to the silent peer, and at least 1 registry line of the
probe's manifest. A variant that counted nothing would fail v1.33.13+k3s2 on "never asked the silent
peer", as run 6 did.

## Result

- 1.31 line: no release after v1.31.14+k3s1 exists.
- 1.32 line: no release passes both, with either witness. v1.32.13+k3s1 passes `shared` and hangs on
  the silent peer. v1.32.10 and v1.32.11+k3s1 fail `shared`. v1.32.9 hangs on the silent peer. The
  other 1.32 releases share a fork with a measured release that hangs, or carry an older fork that
  bounds only the lookup.
- 1.33 line: v1.33.13+k3s2 passes `shared` (2 of 2) and passes `silent-peer` with the data witness
  (2 of 2). With the witness as shipped, it fails `silent-peer` only on the witness (1 of 1). By
  source, it is the only 1.33 release that can pass both: the others either lack `labelContent` or
  leave a blob request to a peer unbounded.

## What SOV-721 takes from this

1. The release is v1.33.13+k3s2. The image list file of the touches becomes
   `infra/providers/_shared/k3s-images/v1.33.13+k3s2.txt` (sha256 above), and every pin of the
   touches names v1.33.13+k3s2. The image digest above is what this host's Docker recorded. The
   digest of the multi-platform index pinned in `tests/e2e/k3s-bootstrap-flags.sh` is read again by
   SOV-721.
2. `scripts/check-registry-drain.sh` (a path of SOV-721) takes the data witness of line 787 and the
   words of lines 809 to 811, as in `scripts/check-registry-drain-datapackets.sh` on `spike/SOV-722`.
   `shared` is not changed. The silent-peer-control release stays v1.31.8+k3s1.
3. The move is two minors, 1.31 to 1.33. The default of `k3s_version` applies to new installs only.
   A kept cluster moves one minor at a time through SOV-720's version route, so its 1.32 step runs a
   release that fails one of the two cases. The order of SOV-725 (1.32) and SOV-726 (1.33) after
   SOV-721, and the rule of one kernel move between release candidates, are for the architect.
4. CI decides SOV-721's B1 and B2. This host and GitHub's runner disagreed on spegel v0.4.0's witness
   step. The passing release was not measured on GitHub's runner here.

## Limits

- One host and local Docker, not GitHub's runner. Each passing case ran twice. Each failing release
  ran once per case, except v1.32.13+k3s1's silent-peer, which ran three times.
- Cases `control`, `restart`, `roll`, `roll-control` and `silent-peer-control` were not run on
  v1.33.13+k3s2. SOV-721's CI runs them.
- "registry log lines of the probe's manifest" is the harness's `served` count, which counts log
  lines. The stand-in registry writes two lines per request, so 4 lines are 2 requests (HEAD and GET).
- The data witness takes any packet of 100 bytes or more for a request. In the 1 s pull window, no
  other traffic to that port from rd-agent-2 was seen in the logs.
- The 1.34 line was not measured. By `go.mod`, v1.34.10+k3s1 and later carry spegel v0.7.2-k3s1.
- Release facts that were not measured come from source read at each tag, not from a run: the table
  rows for v1.32.0 to v1.32.8, v1.32.11+k3s3, v1.32.12, and every 1.33 release other than
  v1.33.13+k3s2. v1.33.0's fork v0.1.1-k3s1 was not read.
- `labelContent` was read at v1.31.14+k3s1, v1.32.10+k3s1, v1.32.11+k3s1, v1.32.11+k3s3,
  v1.32.12+k3s1, v1.32.13+k3s1, v1.33.6+k3s1, v1.33.7+k3s1, v1.33.7+k3s3, v1.33.8+k3s1,
  v1.33.13+k3s1 and v1.33.13+k3s2. The other rows assume that it is absent before the first release
  that has it and kept after it.
