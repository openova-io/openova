# SOV-343: found and not fixed

- Cutover step 06 removes the origin's login from the pull Secret at its end (Phase 3), and the provider pod mounts that Secret as the credential of its CLI configuration. Steps 07, 09 and 10 run before step 11 (orders 7 to 9 against 10), so from the end of step 06 until step 11 has moved the CLI configuration, a `tofu init` of a cloud Workspace reads the origin's mirror without a credential for it, and the origin's packages are private. A Workspace that reconciles in that window is `Synced=False` until it reconciles after step 11. SOV-344's baseline counts only Workspaces that are Ready and Synced before the hold. No package owns moving the CLI configuration before the login is removed.
- The release route's prewarm runs the candidate's step 03, whose Phase A5 copies the packages the live ConfigMap `crossplane-system/opentofu-providers` lists, not those of the candidate's `clusters/_template/infrastructure/providers/base/opentofu-providers.yaml`. A candidate that lists a new provider version reaches the Sovereign's registry only through the day-two reconciler's OpenTofu leg, in its delivery window (mode `warm`, or a request). SOV-230's closure copy, which replaces step 03's body, carries the list.
