# BSS-040 — Delete core/marketplace-api

## In plain words

**The problem today.** The chart still installs a marketplace back end that no screen calls: the storefront sends every request to the gateway of the Organization services. Its Go module, its build workflow and its image pin are kept up to date for nothing, nine of its routes sit in the table of routes that change state, and guards and the platform verifier still expect its Pod.

**What changes.** The module, its templates and its build workflow are deleted. The image pin, the workflow legs, the route table entries, the guard lists and the verifier line that name them are removed in the same change, and the list of deleted paths keeps them out.

**Done means:**

- The folder core/marketplace-api, the folder of its chart templates and its build workflow no longer exist, and the list of deleted paths names all three, so a pull request that brings one back turns red.
- The umbrella chart, rendered with the storefront switched on, holds no Deployment, Service or Secret of the deleted back end, and still holds the storefront.
- The chart values hold no image pin for it, and the two image-pin guards pass without it.
- No workflow, Dependabot entry or script names the deleted module, and the test workflow has no matrix leg for it.
- The table of routes that change state has no entry of the deleted component, and its guard passes.
- The chart test of the mail relay, the single-replica guard, the polling guard and the two secret guards pass, and none of their lists names a deleted file.
- The platform verifier no longer waits for a Pod of the deleted back end.
