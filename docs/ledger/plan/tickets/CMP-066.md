# CMP-066 — Delete what is left: the stub and the test-only set; the access policy gets its suite

## In plain words

**The problem today.** The policy chart still carries a stub template that renders nothing, and the platform chart carries a test-only set of nineteen policies of the legacy kind that nothing uses. Together they hold the count of legacy policy objects at twenty-one. The policy that keeps a grant inside its own Organization has no test suite at all.

**What changes.** The stub and the test-only set are deleted, which leaves two legacy policy objects in the tree. The access policy gets a test suite written on its current form and run as a developer and as an owner, so every check of the compliance model has a suite.

**Done means:**

- The stub template and the test-only policy set are gone, and the list of deleted paths names both.
- The legacy policy guard counts two objects: the access policy and the image-signature rule.
- The suite of the access policy holds the current render of that policy and passes ten tests in the policy chart tests.
- No check of the compliance model is listed as having no suite.
- The chart function test no longer names the stub, and its render test passes.
