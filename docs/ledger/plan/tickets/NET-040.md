# NET-040 — Remove the unwired firewall and the unused SPIRE chart

## In plain words

**The problem today.** Every Sovereign installs a web application firewall that no request ever passes through: nothing routes traffic to it, yet the network policies give it a place on the public entry path. The SPIRE workload-identity chart is in the tree although no Sovereign installs it. The Cilium values call node-to-node WireGuard encryption mTLS, which it is not. Together they make the platform look more protected than it is.

**What changes.** The firewall's kit slot, its chart, its catalogue entry, its logo and every list that names it are removed, and so is the SPIRE chart with its lists. The Cilium values say what WireGuard does: it encrypts traffic between nodes and does not authenticate workloads.

**Done means:**

- No Sovereign installs the firewall any more: kit slot 35, its chart folder and its logo are gone, and the list of deleted paths names each of them, so a pull request that brings one back turns red.
- The SPIRE chart folder is gone and listed as deleted, and no kit test or catalogue list expects it.
- The catalogue seed, the generated catalogue and the generated inventories name neither Blueprint, and each is in sync with its generator.
- The two network policy charts render no rule for the firewall namespace, and the cutover list, the kit expectations and the readiness verifier no longer name the firewall.
- The network-guard, service-model and upstream-literal guards pass with their baselines lowered by what the deleted files counted.
- The Cilium values no longer use the word mTLS and say that WireGuard encrypts traffic between nodes and does not authenticate workloads.

The rest of this ticket is the exact instruction for the model that does the work.
