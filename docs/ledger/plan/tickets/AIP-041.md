# AIP-041 — The quota model of an Organization follows the gateway Pod without the metering sidecar

## In plain words

**The problem today.** The model gateway Pod of an Organization no longer runs the metering sidecar, but the quota model of the org-controller still reserves room for it. Every Organization's platform quota is 1000m of CPU limit larger than its platform pods can use, and the guards that compare the model with the chart were relaxed to allow that.

**What changes.** The quota model reserves the gateway Pod as the chart renders it, without the sidecar. The guards compare model and chart as equalities again, and the documents state the new quota figures.

**Done means:**

- The org-controller renders the platform-quota of an Organization with 5075m and 7792Mi of requests and 5500m and 9344Mi of limits, on every plan.
- The quota model of the gateway Pod has two containers, newapi and sandbox-bridge, and a test fails when the chart turns the metering sidecar on again.
- The quota tests of the org-controller, the provisioning service and the bootstrap API fail when the model and the chart differ in either direction.
- The architecture documents state the same quota figures as the code.
- After the update of the environment, a gateway Pod is admitted under the smaller quota at 700m of CPU limit, and that reading is recorded on the tracking issue.

The rest of this ticket is the exact instruction for the model that does the work.
