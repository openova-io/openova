# NET-081 — One producer of the Organization kubeconfig

## In plain words

**The problem today.** The provisioning service used to copy each Organization's cluster credentials into two places itself, and was given the right to create, change and delete Secrets for that copy. The copy and its repair loop are gone, but the rights are still granted in three places, and comments still describe the copy as live.

**What changes.** The provisioning service account may only read Secrets. The three places that grant its rights say the same, and the comments name the one producer that remains.

**Done means:**

- The platform chart grants the provisioning service account no create, update, patch or delete on Secrets, in flux-system or anywhere else.
- The Role that the provisioning service renders for an Organization namespace and the Role that the organization controller renders for it grant the same read-only Secret rule.
- The tests of the provisioning service and of the organization controller fail if a Secret write verb comes back.
- No source file under core names the deleted kubeconfig copy function.
- The deleted reconciler file is listed as deleted, so it cannot come back.

The rest of this ticket is the exact instruction for the model that does the work.

## 2. End goal
The Organization kubeconfig Secret `tenant-<slug>-kubeconfig` has exactly one producer: the Organization's boundary tree that the organization controller renders (`core/controllers/organization/internal/gitops/region_local.go`). The vCluster writes it in `<slug>` and bp-reflector copies it into flux-system. The provisioning service only waits, read-only, until both copies hold it. Its former copy (`mirrorVClusterKubeconfig`), its 60-second repair loop and its teardown delete were removed from the code already; this package removes what is left of them: the Secret write rights of the service account `org-services/provisioning` and the comments that still describe the copy. A person who renders the platform chart sees `verbs: ["get", "list", "watch"]` on both Secret rules of the service account; a person who renders an Organization sees the same verbs on the Secret rule of the Role `provisioning-tenant`.

## 3. The model (the law)
1. One writer per Secret. The service account `org-services/provisioning` writes no Secret through the Kubernetes API. Secrets that the service renders into Git (database passwords) reach the cluster through Flux, not through this account.
2. The Secret rule of every Role or ClusterRole bound to that account is `verbs: ["get", "list", "watch"]`. There are three: the ClusterRole `org-provisioning` and the Role `flux-system/org-provisioning-fluxsystem` in `products/catalyst/chart/templates/org-services/provisioning.yaml`, and the Role `<slug>/provisioning-tenant`.
3. The Role `<slug>/provisioning-tenant` has two emitters that must agree: `generateProvisioningTenantRBAC` in `core/services/provisioning/gitops/gitops.go` (committed by `GenerateAll` as `provisioning-rbac.yaml`) and `provisioningRBACTemplate` in `core/controllers/organization/internal/gitops/manifests.go` (rendered into `vcluster/host-apps/` on a Sovereign, where the service's own file is dropped). Both get the same read-only Secret rule. Every other rule of both Roles stays as it is.
4. The tests of both emitters fail when a Secret write verb comes back.
5. No file under `core` names `mirrorVClusterKubeconfig`. Comments that described the copy as live are corrected.
6. Deleted: the Secret verbs `create`, `update`, `patch` (both Roles `provisioning-tenant`) and `create`, `update`, `patch`, `delete` (Role `org-provisioning-fluxsystem`). The file `core/services/provisioning/handlers/kubeconfig_reconciler.go`, already gone from the tree, is appended to `docs/ledger/deleted-paths.txt`.
7. No Go behaviour changes. No chart `version:`, `appVersion:`, pin or image tag changes.
