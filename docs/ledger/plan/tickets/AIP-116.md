# AIP-116 — Kit slot 82 and the catalog seed pin bp-axon

## In plain words

**The problem today.** The chart of Axon, the simulation back end of the model plane, is published, but no Sovereign installs it: no kit slot names it and the catalogue does not know it. Its credential lives in OpenBao, and the network policy in front of OpenBao drops every dial from the namespace Axon would run in, so even an installed Axon could never start.

**What changes.** Every Sovereign installs Axon from the kit at the published version: one running copy in the primary region and none elsewhere, starting with an empty credential that it reports as absent. The catalogue lists it as an unlisted Blueprint at the same version, and its namespace may reach OpenBao.

**Done means:**

- The kit holds a slot that installs Axon at the chart version the registry holds, in the namespace axon, after OpenBao, the CSI driver of mounted secrets and Cilium.
- In the primary region one Axon pod runs and in every other region none; on a fresh install the slot asks for an empty credential, which the pod reads as absent while its health probes answer.
- The catalogue holds Axon as an unlisted Blueprint at the same version as the slot.
- The policy in front of OpenBao admits the namespace axon, and no other policy changes.
- The generated lists of slots, components, catalogue and installer rings name the new slot and are current.

The rest of this ticket is the exact instruction for the model that does the work.
