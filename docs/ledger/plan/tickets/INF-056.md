# INF-056 — Check INF-G01 restarts the node of the registry's front and requires every moved Pod back and no container waiting on an image

## In plain words

**The problem today.** The acceptance check of coordinated node restarts restarts the first worker by name, which need not run the registry front through which every node pulls its images after the cutover. It judges the recovery by the HelmReleases, which stay Ready for many minutes while Pods cannot pull their images. On a fresh two-region install on 20 November the drain of the one node that held the registry front left 14 of 21 moved Pods unable to start, and this check would have passed.

**What changes.** The check restarts the worker that runs the registry front, so it makes the drain that broke that install. It passes only when every workload the drain moved is Ready again and no container of the region waits on an image.

**Done means:**

- In each region the check restarts the worker that runs a Ready endpoint of the registry front; when no worker runs one, the first worker by name that runs workloads, and its record says which rule chose.
- The check fails when a controller whose Pod the drain moved has fewer Ready Pods at the time limit than before the restart, and names it with its image.
- The check fails when a container of the region waits on an image at the time limit, even when every HelmRelease is Ready, and names the container and its image.
- The check touches no node when a container of the region already waits on an image before it starts.
- The tests of the check run without a cluster, with a failing case for each new rule, among them the drain of the registry front's node whose replacement cannot pull its image.

The rest of this ticket is the exact instruction for the model that does the work.

3. **The node choice (step 3).** The check reads the nodes, every Pod of the region once (`kubectl get -o json --request-timeout=15s pods -A`; it replaces the two reads with a field selector of today), and the EndpointSlices of Service `harbor/harbor` (`endpointslices.discovery.k8s.io -n harbor -l kubernetes.io/service-name=harbor`). A node runs a Ready endpoint of the front when one of its endpoints has that `nodeName` and a condition `ready` that is true or not set (an unset condition counts as ready, as the API defines it). The chosen worker is the first worker by name that runs a Ready endpoint of the front; when no worker runs one (the Service has none, or only a control-plane node runs it), the first worker by name that runs a workload Pod, as today. The evidence `chosen_by` names the rule, `registry_front` the nodes. A read of the EndpointSlices that fails fails the step and touches no node.
4. **No image wait before.** In the same step, after the choice, a container (init or main) of any Pod of the region that waits with reason `ErrImagePull` or `ImagePullBackOff` fails the step and no node is touched: the drain could not be judged. The evidence `image_waits_before` lists them.
5. **The controllers of the moved Pods (step 3).** For each workload Pod that runs on the chosen worker the check records its controller: the owner reference with `controller: true`. A ReplicaSet whose name is a Deployment's name, `-` and the Pod's label `pod-template-hash` counts as that Deployment (the name the Deployment controller gives its ReplicaSets), so a Pod that comes back under a new ReplicaSet of the Deployment counts. A Pod of a Job is not judged (a Job ends its Pods by design) and neither is a Pod without a controller (nothing brings it back). For each controller it records how many Pods of the region with that controller are Ready (condition `Ready` `True`, not being deleted), and the images of the moved Pod. The evidence `controllers_moved` holds the counts.
6. **The recovery (step 7).** When the worker has restarted, no node is unschedulable and every node is Ready, the check reads every Pod of the region once more. From that reading it judges, as today, that no workload Pod of step 3 is still on the worker (else it fails at once). The step passes only when, besides kured's DaemonSet fully ready and every HelmRelease of step 5 Ready again, every controller of rule 5 has at least as many Ready Pods as it had in step 3 and no container of any Pod of the region waits with `ErrImagePull` or `ImagePullBackOff`. A container that waits fails the step even when every HelmRelease is Ready. Otherwise the readings go on; a read that fails is no observation. At the time limit the reason names each controller with fewer Ready Pods (`<Kind> <namespace>/<name> has <n> of <m> Ready Pods (<images>)`) and each waiting container (`<namespace>/<pod>/<container> <reason> <image>`). The evidence `controllers_not_ready_again` and `image_waits` hold them.
7. **The manifest entry stays.** `docs/ledger/gates.yaml` is not changed: a change of `asserts` needs `Work-Package: PLAN` (rule 7 of `python3 scripts/acceptance-gate.py validate`), which is the architect's plan data, as INF-052 and pull requests 7919 and 7921 did. Field 9 gives the text for that plan change.
8. **Nothing is deleted** but the two read helpers of the check that the one reading of every Pod replaces (`workloads` with a field selector, `stayed`).
