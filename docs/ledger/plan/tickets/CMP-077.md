## In plain words

**What changes.** The policy is written in the new mutating kind, in the same file and under the same name, and a new suite of nine cases gives the same results and the same patched objects on the legacy policy and on the new one. - The policy chart renders stamp-cilium-enforced-label as a MutatingPolicy, and no template of the mutate policies holds the legacy kind.

- The legacy policy guard counts one object fewer: 24 on the base of this ticket.
