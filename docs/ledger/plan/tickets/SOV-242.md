# SOV-242 — The cutover repeats a failed step a few times with a growing pause, then stops in a failed state the sovereign-admin resumes

## In plain words

**The problem today.** When a step of the sovereignty cutover fails, the reconciler of the API starts the cutover again every two minutes, without a limit. A failed egress test therefore cut the outbound traffic of a region again and again until it happened to pass. The console shows a failed step but offers no way to go on.

**What changes.** A failed step is repeated at most three times, each time after a longer pause. Then the cutover stays stopped at that step, the console says so and names the step, and only the sovereign-admin's Resume cutover button takes it further.

**Done means:**

- A step that fails on every run is repeated exactly three times in six hours, after pauses of at least 5, 10 and 20 minutes, and never again after that.
- After the third repetition the status record of the cutover names the step and says that the automatic repetitions are spent.
- A repetition that started no run does not count.
- The console shows the next automatic repetition while one is pending, and shows the stopped step with a Resume cutover button when none is pending.
- Resume cutover calls the start route, which is recorded as a retry call and gives the step a fresh set of repetitions.

The rest of this ticket is the exact instruction for the model that does the work.

## 2. End goal
The level-triggered cutover reconciler of catalyst-api (`products/catalyst/bootstrap/api/internal/handler/cutover_reconciler.go`, a pass every 2 minutes) no longer re-fires a failed step on every pass. Its decision moves into a new package, `products/catalyst/bootstrap/api/internal/cutoverreconcile`, whose `Pass` keeps the old behaviour while no step has failed and bounds the repetitions of a failed step: repetition n waits `5m×2^(n-1)` after the later of the step's failure and the previous repetition, at most 3 repetitions; then the record is marked halted and nothing is fired. The record is six keys of the status ConfigMap (`autoRetryStep`, `autoRetryCount`, `autoRetryLimit`, `autoRetryAt`, `autoRetryNextAt`, `autoRetryHalted`), so a Pod restart neither resets nor repeats the count. The start route `POST /api/v1/sovereign/cutover/start`, which already records a retry call, clears the record. The sovereign-admin console reads the record: the progress card shows "repetition n of 3 at HH:MM" while one is pending, and "Cutover stopped at step ..." with a **Resume cutover** button (it calls the start route) when none is. A person sees it this way: a cutover step that keeps failing is tried three more times within about 35 minutes and then waits, named in the console, for the sovereign-admin.

## 3. The model (the law)
1. **Decide is pure.** `cutoverreconcile.Decide(status, now, policy)` reads only the status map. No failed step: `None` (fire as before). First sight of a failed step (`autoRetryStep` holds another step or nothing): open the record with count 0 and wait. Count below the limit and the pause elapsed: `Fire`, recording count+1 before the fire. Pause not elapsed: `Wait`. Count at the limit, a count that does not parse, or no time to count from: `Halt`, recording `autoRetryHalted: "true"`. Already halted: `Halt`, recording nothing.
2. **The pause grows.** `DefaultPolicy` is 3 repetitions with a first pause of 5 minutes: 5, 10, 20 minutes. The pause counts from the later of `autoRetryAt` (the last repetition) and `step.<step>.finishedAt` (the failure the engine records).
3. **Counted before fired, given back when nothing ran.** A repetition is recorded before the engine is fired; a record that cannot be written fires nothing. A fire that started no engine run (already running, already complete, below the chart floor, an error) writes the previous record back.
4. **The pass keeps its old rules.** No fire before the handover seal, after the durable cutover-complete seal, on a read error or without the cutover chart. `Pass` publishes the status ConfigMap to the console's event stream after it writes the record.
5. **Only the sovereign-admin goes on.** The start route clears the record (`cutoverreconcile.Cleared()`) after it records the retry call and before the engine runs; a later failure gets a fresh budget. Nothing else clears it.
6. **The handler package does not grow.** `internal/handler` keeps `runCutoverReconcilePass` as the adapter that wires the engine's operations (`sovereignHandoverComplete`, `sovereignCutoverComplete`, `readCutoverStatus`, `patchCutoverStatus`, `fireCutoverEngine`, the event bus) into `Pass`. The record is written through the existing `patchCutoverStatus`; the new package makes no cluster call. The baselines of `scripts/check-api-structure.py` (`handler_lines`) and `scripts/check-lifecycle-ratchets.sh` (`sequencer_lines`) go down by the 13 lines that left; no method is added to `Handler`.
7. **The console.** `parseCutoverStatus` reads the `autoRetry*` keys from the top level (the flat ConfigMap frame of the event stream) or from `raw` (the GET /status body), and only when `autoRetryStep` equals `failedStep`. `CutoverProgressCard` takes `onResume`, `resuming` and `resumeError`; `SovereigntyCard` passes the hook's `startCutover`, `starting` and `startError`.
