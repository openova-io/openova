# CMP-094 — Vulnerability scanning produces reports in every region

## In plain words

**The problem today.** On a two-region Sovereign the primary region holds no vulnerability report, no software bill of materials and no exposed-secret report for its 307 Pods, while the secondary region holds 132, 167 and 166 of them, and configuration auditing works in both. In the chart, every finished scan Job is kept for five minutes and the operator then never deletes it, so each one holds one of the two scan slots, and a workload whose old Job is still there gets no new scan. The list of namespaces the operator should skip sits at a key the upstream chart does not read, so it skips none.

**What changes.** Scan Jobs get no time-to-live, the upstream default, so the operator deletes each Job as soon as it has read it and the two slots are free for the next scans. The namespace scope moves to the keys the upstream chart reads, and a chart test proves both in the rendered configuration, also with the values the kit passes.

**Done means:**

- The rendered operator configuration carries an empty scan Job time-to-live and a scan concurrency of two, for the chart alone and with the values of the kit's trivy slot.
- The rendered operator container skips kube-system, kube-public and kube-node-lease and scans every other namespace.
- The chart test tests/scan-job-lifecycle.sh passes on the pull request under the yq and Helm of the release, and it fails on the values of main.
- The operator's memory, the scan Jobs' limits, the chart's version line and the kit's trivy slot do not change.
- The pull request states that the cause of the missing reports in the primary region is not proven without a cluster, and what was ruled out.

The rest of this ticket is the exact instruction for the model that does the work.
