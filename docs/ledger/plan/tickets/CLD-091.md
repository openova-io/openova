# CLD-091 — The local pack connects the meshes of its two regions

## In plain words

**The problem today.** The local provider pack starts two regions, but nothing connects their Cilium meshes. On a Sovereign the console connects them after the first phase; the pack has no such console, so the agents of one region never learn the nodes of the other. The suite's mesh step therefore fails on the pack, and the gate suite cannot pass there.

**What changes.** With two regions, the pack writes the mesh peer entries and host aliases of each region the way the console does, restarts Cilium and waits until every agent reports the other region ready. Its discovery then also judges the suite's mesh step.

**Done means:**

- With two regions, the local pack is up only when every Cilium agent of each region reports the other region ready.
- The pack's discovery passes only when the suite's mesh step D0.10 passes, as well as D0.1 and D0.2.
- The check conformance-local is green on the pull request, and its log shows the mesh ready and step D0.10 passed.
- A wrong peer entry, a wrong host alias or a wrong readiness verdict turns the script's self-test red.
- A pack of one region does not touch the mesh.

The rest of this ticket is the exact instruction for the model that does the work.

## 3. The model (the law)
1. **The pack does what the console does after phase 1.** On a Sovereign the console that provisioned it (its parent) connects the meshes in `AutoEstablishClusterMesh` (`products/catalyst/bootstrap/api/internal/handler/clustermesh.go`) once phase 1 is done. The pack has no parent, so `up` does the same three things itself, in every region, when the pack has two regions:
   - the Secret `kube-system/cilium-clustermesh` holds, per peer region, four entries: `<peer cluster name>` (the endpoint configuration, byte for byte what `buildPeerConfigBlob` writes, with the endpoint `https://<peer cluster name>.mesh.cilium.io:<dial port>`), `<peer>-ca.crt` (the `ca.crt` of the peer's Secret `kube-system/cilium-ca`), `<peer>.crt` and `<peer>.key` (the `tls.crt` and `tls.key` of the peer's Secret `kube-system/clustermesh-apiserver-remote-cert`). It holds nothing of its own region.
   - the DaemonSet `kube-system/cilium` carries one host alias per peer: `<peer cluster name>.mesh.cilium.io` at the peer's mesh endpoint address. The server certificate of every clustermesh-apiserver covers `*.mesh.cilium.io`.
   - the DaemonSet `cilium` and the Deployments `cilium-operator` and `clustermesh-apiserver` restart, the three targets the console restarts.
2. **Names and addresses come from the module.** The cluster name, the mesh endpoint address and the dial port of each region are read from the output `network_registry` of `infra/providers/local` (its `clusterName` and `meshEndpoint`). The module gives the same cluster name to Cilium, and the mesh proxy listens on the dial port at the machine address. No value is written into the script.
3. **The key material passes through variables and pipes only.** The CA, the client certificates and their keys are read with `kubectl` inside the machines and written with `kubectl apply` from a pipe; they are never written to a file of the runner and never passed as a command argument.
4. **The wait reads what the agents report.** `up` reads, from every Cilium agent of every region, what `cilium-dbg status -o jsonpath={range .cluster-mesh.clusters[*]}{.name}={.ready};{end}` prints, the read of `products/catalyst/bootstrap/api/internal/meshagents/meshagents.go`. It goes on only when every agent of each region reports every peer cluster with `true`. An agent that reports no peer, `false`, or only another cluster, and a region with no agent, are not ready. The whole mesh step has 10 minutes; at the end it prints what each agent reported and fails.
5. **One region, no mesh.** With one region `up` does not touch the mesh.
6. **Discovery judges D0.10.** `discovery` runs `sovereign-acceptance run --env local-pack --run-in local --journeys '' --manifest ''` with the console address, the deployment, the session and both kubeconfigs. A run that requests no journey leaves out the discovery steps D0.3 to D0.5, which only the journeys need (FND-131), so D0.6 and D0.10 run; it requests no gate check and writes no record. `discovery` passes when the step lines of D0.1, D0.2 and D0.10 are printed with `PASS`, within 10 minutes, as before. The other steps are printed and not judged.
7. **Evidence.** `collect` also writes what each Cilium agent reports of its peers (`<region>/mesh.txt`) and copies the last verdicts of the mesh wait (`mesh-<region>.txt`).
8. **Everything else stays.** The commands, their exit codes, the env file, the kubeconfig files, the session, the kit core and every time limit of `up` stay. No node port: the agents dial the mesh proxy on its host port; nothing creates a Service.
9. **The self-test proves each rule can fail.** `--self-test` covers the peer document (a region the registry does not hold, a region without a mesh endpoint and an empty key are refused), the Secret (the peer's entries only, the endpoint blob byte for byte), the host aliases, the peers of a region, the readiness verdict (each of the five cases of law 4) and the discovery verdict and its call of the suite (law 6).
