# SEC-196 — Hand-over identity sovereign-ops

## In plain words

**The problem today.** The hand-over Job, which moves a Sovereign's infrastructure state into the Sovereign, has no identity of its own in the secrets store. It can use the state key, but it cannot write the hand-over inputs or the cloud credentials, and it cannot read or remove the hand-over archive, short of a broad grant.

**What changes.** The secrets store gets one policy and one login role for the hand-over Job. With them the Job writes and reads its inputs and the cloud credentials, reads and removes the hand-over archive and uses the state key, and it reaches nothing else.

**Done means:**

- The hand-over Job writes, updates and reads back its inputs and a cloud credential in the secrets store.
- The hand-over Job reads the hand-over archive, cannot rewrite it, and removes it with every version.
- The hand-over Job is refused every other value of the platform's prefix, the parent's cloud path, the sign-in values, the listing of keys, the store of mounted values and the backup of the state key.
- The hand-over Job encrypts and decrypts with the state key.
- Without a job identity the chart writes the policy and no role, and a first job identity without a namespace stops the chart from rendering.

The rest of this ticket is the exact instruction for the model that does the work.

## 3. The model (the law)
1. **Policy.** One op `write sys/policies/acl/sovereign-ops`, always rendered, whose text is exactly these four paths on the kv mount (`kubernetesAuth.kvMountPath`, default `secret`), in this order: `<kv>/data/catalyst/tofu/*` with `["create", "update", "read"]`; `<kv>/data/catalyst/cloud/*` with `["create", "update", "read"]`; `<kv>/data/catalyst/tofu-phase0-archive` with `["read"]`; `<kv>/metadata/catalyst/tofu-phase0-archive` with `["read", "delete"]`. No `list`, no `delete` on a data path, no other path.
2. **Role.** One op `write auth/<kubernetesAuth.mountPath>/role/sovereign-ops` binding service account `sovereign-ops` in the namespace of the FIRST entry of `stateKey.jobIdentities` (default `catalyst`, `platform/openbao/chart/values.yaml` lines 279 to 281), audience `openbao`, policies `["sovereign-ops", "tofu-state"]`, `ttl` 15m, `max_ttl` 1h. Later entries of the list do not change it.
3. **Empty and broken lists.** As for the role `tofu-state`, an empty `stateKey.jobIdentities` renders no role `sovereign-ops`; the policy stays. A first entry whose namespace is not a DNS label fails the render with the message `stateKey.jobIdentities: the first entry has no namespace for role sovereign-ops`.
4. **Not covered.** The parent's cloud path `secret/catalyst/mothership/cloud/<provider>` is not in the policy: its writer is not decided in this package, and the live case L10 proves that this identity is refused a write there.
5. **Order.** The policy op comes after `tofu-state-mint` and before `sovereign-admin` among the policies; the role comes after `tofu-state` and `tofu-state-backup` and before the producer roles. The reconciler file `files/openbao-config-reconcile.sh` does not change; it applies the two new ops as every write op.
6. **Not this identity.** The human group `sovereign-ops` that `templates/sso-configure-deployment.yaml` maps to policy `sovereign-admin` is an identity group, not this ACL policy, and does not change.
7. **Tests.** `config-desired-render.sh` gains case C9 (shell, yq and jq; no line of Python); `tests/e2e/bp-openbao-config-live.sh` gains case L10. No chart version and no value changes; the release writer bumps the version after the merge.
