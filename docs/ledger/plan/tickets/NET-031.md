# NET-031 — Stop reflecting the object-storage Secret to retired namespaces

## In plain words

**The problem today.** At first boot a Sovereign is told to copy its object-storage credential into three namespaces (mgmt, dmz, rtz) that no longer exist. If someone creates a namespace with one of those names, it receives the credential.

**What changes.** The instruction to copy is removed. The credential stays where the registry and the backup tool read it.

**Done means:**

- The boot file no longer names the three namespaces for this credential.
- The credential itself and its keys are unchanged.
- The three other credentials that are copied on purpose keep their settings.
- The boot file still renders and stays under its size limit.

The rest of this ticket is the exact instruction for the model that does the work.
