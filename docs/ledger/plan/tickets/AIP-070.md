# AIP-070 — Delete the hollow gateway charts

## In plain words

**The problem today.** Two model-gateway charts sit in the platform folder although nothing installs them: neither has a bootstrap-kit slot, the adapter pins an image that no workflow builds, and the gateway chart only wraps an upstream proxy that the platform does not use. One of them is still offered in the Sovereign's catalogue, so a person can pick a Blueprint that does not work.

**What changes.** Both folders are deleted and recorded as deleted, so they cannot come back unnoticed. The catalogue, the exclusion list, the one Blueprint that named the gateway as a dependency and three guard baselines are updated to match.

**Done means:**

- The two chart folders are gone, and the list of deleted paths names both, so a pull request that brings one back turns red.
- The catalogue that the console embeds and the seed that a Sovereign installs no longer hold either Blueprint, and both are what the generator writes.
- The list of Blueprints that the seed leaves out no longer names the adapter, and LibreChat no longer declares the deleted gateway as a dependency.
- The agent-plane, network and service-model guards pass, and none of their baselines names a deleted file.
- Every other Blueprint, including NewAPI and LibreChat, is still in the repository.

The rest of this ticket is the exact instruction for the model that does the work.
