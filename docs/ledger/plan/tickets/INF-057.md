# INF-057 — Check INF-G01 requires every moved workload back within 180 seconds of leaving the worker

## In plain words

**The problem today.** The acceptance check of coordinated node restarts judges the return of the workloads that a drain moves only at one time limit, 50 minutes after the restart is requested. On a fresh install on 21 November image copies between workers stalled for 1 to 17 minutes; the moved workloads came back long after a healthy drain brings them back, and the check would still have passed. On a healthy drain of 20 November every moved Pod was Ready again within 76 seconds of its creation.

**What changes.** Each workload that the drain moved is timed from the moment its Pods leave the worker and must have its Ready Pods back within 180 seconds; past that the check fails at once and names the workload, its images and the seconds. The 50-minute limit stays for the restart of the node, and the record of a passing check gives every moved workload's seconds.

**Done means:**

- The check fails at once when a workload whose Pod the drain moved has its Ready Pods back only 185 seconds after that Pod left the worker, naming the workload, its image and the seconds, even when the node is back and every HelmRelease is Ready.
- A workload back 175 seconds after its Pod left the worker passes.
- The seconds count from the reading in which the Pod left the worker, not from the request for the restart: a drain that starts 20 minutes after the request and whose Pods are back 60 seconds after leaving passes.
- A Pod that is being deleted on the worker counts as having left it.
- The record of a passing check gives, for every moved workload, its seconds to be back, each at most 180.
- Every rule of the check before this change still holds, the 50-minute limit for the node's restart among them, and the tests of the check run without a cluster with a failing case for each new rule.

The rest of this ticket is the exact instruction for the model that does the work.

## 2. End goal
Gate check INF-G01 (coordinated reboot, `tests/acceptance/sovereign/internal/suite/check_inf_g01.go`) times every controller whose Pod the drain of the restarted worker moved: from the first reading in which none of its Pods of the node choice is on the worker any more (gone, being deleted or ended) to the first reading in which it has as many Ready Pods as it had at the node choice. That must take at most 180 seconds, about twice the slowest Pod of a healthy drain. Past that the step fails at once and names the controller, its images and the seconds, so an image copy that stalls for minutes fails the check instead of being met inside the time limit of the node's restart (period + lock release delay + 15 minutes, 50 minutes with the kit's settings), which stays. The record of the step gives every moved controller's seconds to be back. The README of the suite describes the check as it now is. The tests of the check need no cluster and run on every pull request.

## 3. The model (the law)
1. **The files are fixed.** The change is the block `INF-057.patch` at the end of field 20, applied by work step 4. It changes `check_inf_g01.go`, `check_inf_g01_test.go` and `tests/acceptance/sovereign/README.md` and nothing else; the three files have the checksums of work step 6 afterwards.
2. **One constant.** `rebootBack = 180 * time.Second`, with a comment that gives the measurement it rests on. Nothing else of the check states the bound.
3. **When a controller has left the worker.** For each controller recorded at the node choice (step 3: the owner reference marked controller; a ReplicaSet of a Deployment counts as the Deployment; Pods of a Job and Pods without a controller are not judged, as today) the check keeps the keys (namespace, name and uid) of its Pods that ran on the worker. The controller has left the worker in the first reading in which none of those Pods is a workload Pod on the worker: each is gone, carries a deletion timestamp, or is no longer `Pending` or `Running`. That is the rule by which the check already judges that the drain moved the Pods off (`stayed`).
4. **When it is back.** It is back in the first reading, at or after the one in which it left, in which it has at least as many Ready Pods in the region (condition `Ready` `True`, not being deleted) as it had at the node choice. The seconds from the reading in which it left to the one in which it is back, rounded up to a whole second, go into the evidence `controllers_seconds_back` of step 7, once per controller.
5. **The bound.** In a reading in which a controller has left, is not yet recorded back and more than `rebootBack` has passed since it left, the step fails at once: `<n> of <m> controllers whose Pods the drain moved are not back within 180 seconds of their Pods leaving <worker>: <items>`, an item being `<Kind> <namespace>/<name> has <r> of <m> Ready Pods after <s> seconds (<images>)`, or `<Kind> <namespace>/<name> has its <m> Ready Pods back after <s> seconds (<images>)` when the reading that first finds it back comes later than the bound. When containers of the region wait on an image (`ErrImagePull`, `ImagePullBackOff`), the reason adds them as the recovery names them.
6. **Every reading.** Step 7 reads every Pod of the region in every reading of the nodes, not only once the worker is back, so that the moment the Pods leave is observed during the drain. A reading of the Pods that fails is no observation: it marks nothing and counts as a failed reading.
7. **What stays as it is.** Every rule of the check before this ticket stays, with the same reasons: journey J1 first; one region after the other; the settings from kured's arguments; the install hold; the window rule of the upgrade Plans; kured kept off control-plane nodes; the node choice by the registry front; no image wait before the reboot; the wait for the reboot span; the HelmReleases read before the sentinel Pod; the sentinel Pod and the namespace that is always deleted; every fail-at-once rule of step 7; the recovery (kured, the HelmReleases, every moved controller as many Ready Pods as before, no container waiting on an image) judged at the time limit of period + lock release delay + 15 minutes. The type `rebootCheck` keeps its fields. The check still writes with `kubectl create`, `wait` and `delete` only.
8. **The manifest entry stays.** `docs/ledger/gates.yaml` is not changed: a change of `asserts` is the architect's plan data. Field 9 gives the text for that plan change.
9. **Nothing is deleted.**
