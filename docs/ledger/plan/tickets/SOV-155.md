# SOV-155 — A roll of the registry never removes both copies: harbor-registry rolls one copy at a time, jobservice keeps Recreate

## In plain words

**The problem today.** The registry of a region runs two copies, but its Deployment rolls with Recreate: every roll removes both copies first and starts the new ones after. The sovereignty cutover rolls the registry while outside access is blocked, so the new copy must pull its own image from the registry it replaced; on a fresh install it could not, and the region had no registry from that moment on.

**What changes.** The kit patches the registry's Deployment after the chart is rendered so that it rolls one copy at a time: a new copy starts, becomes ready, and only then is an old copy removed. The job service, the one component whose volume only one node can mount, keeps Recreate, and a chart test judges the roll of every component.

**Done means:**

- In the kit's registry release the registry Deployment rolls with RollingUpdate, no copy unavailable and one extra copy during the roll.
- The front and the core of the registry keep their default rolling, which already keeps both copies, and the job service keeps Recreate.
- A chart test renders the registry chart with the kit's storage, applies the kit's patches and judges the effective roll of every Deployment; it fails on the kit as it was before and on a patch that would roll the job service.
- The chart test prints the registry's effective copies, roll and spread as one line that the drain test of the registry reads.
- The test that keeps two copies, the spread and the disruption budgets of the registry still passes.

The rest of this ticket is the exact instruction for the model that does the work.

## 2. End goal
A forced roll of `harbor-registry` (the restart that cutover step 08 makes under blocked outside access on every fresh install, or an upgrade of bp-harbor that changes the Pod template) keeps a Ready copy of the registry at every moment: the HelmRelease `bp-harbor` of `clusters/_template/bootstrap-kit/19-harbor.yaml` carries one Flux post-render patch that gives the Deployment `harbor-registry` the strategy `RollingUpdate` with `maxUnavailable: 0` and `maxSurge: 1`. `harbor-nginx` and `harbor-core` keep the API default, which at two copies already rolls one extra copy and none unavailable; `harbor-jobservice`, whose job logs sit on a ReadWriteOnce claim, keeps `Recreate` (#5022). ## 3. The model (the law)
1. A Deployment of two or more copies on the registry's pull path rolls with `RollingUpdate` and a resolved `maxUnavailable` below its copies. In the render with the kit's storage these are exactly `harbor-nginx`, `harbor-core` and `harbor-registry`; `harbor-registry` rolls with `maxUnavailable: 0` and `maxSurge: 1`, the numbers the front and the core roll with by default and the kit's precedent (`03-flux.yaml`, source-controller).
2. A Deployment that mounts a ReadWriteOnce claim of the render rolls with `Recreate`. In the render that is exactly `harbor-jobservice`.
3. The upstream subchart has one knob, `harbor.updateStrategy.type`, read by `harbor-registry` and `harbor-jobservice` only; a parent chart cannot change what a packaged subchart renders. The knob stays `Recreate` (for jobservice), and the registry's strategy comes from a `spec.postRenderers` kustomize patch of HelmRelease `bp-harbor` in slot 19, which runs inside helm-controller's render, so a reconcile never reverts it.
4. The patch carries `apiVersion: apps/v1`, `kind: Deployment`, `metadata.name: harbor-registry` and `spec.strategy`, nothing else, and its target is `kind: Deployment`, `name: harbor-registry`. Only then is the yq merge the chart test applies equal to the strategic merge Flux applies.
5. The surge fits the spread: the registry's constraint (maxSkew 1, `kubernetes.io/hostname`, `DoNotSchedule`, `nodeTaintsPolicy: Honor`) counts old and new copies alike, so the third copy lands on an empty node when the region has three or more schedulable nodes, and on either node when it has two (2 and 1). For the length of the roll the extra copy costs the registry's requests.
6. Not changed: the copies, the spread and the budgets of SOV-138; `harbor.updateStrategy.type` of `values.yaml` (only its comment); the chart version, the slot's version pin, `blueprint.yaml` and the catalogue (the release writer sets them after the merge). No NodePort.
