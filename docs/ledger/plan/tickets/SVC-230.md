# SVC-230 — The wizard shows the generated kit list

## In plain words

**The problem today.** The wizard's component step lets a person pick components and product families from a hand-written list. The list drifts from what the platform installs: some entries install nothing, and every Sovereign installs the whole kit anyway. Other console pages take names and logos from the same hand list.

**What changes.** The component step shows, read-only, the rings of the kit and their releases, generated from the kit and the catalogue. The hand list and the product families are gone; the console takes names, logos and dependencies from generated files.

**Done means:**

- The wizard's component step shows the eight rings in their fixed order, foundation and provider marked required, and offers no switch, checkbox or search.
- Every release the step lists is a kit release whose Blueprint has a chart.
- The hand list of components, its integrity test, the product families and the marketplace family and product pages no longer exist.
- One command regenerates the ring list, and a pull request that leaves it stale turns red.
- The console's application pages still show names, logos and dependencies, now read from generated files.
- The service-model counts of hand wizard entries and of wizard entries without a chart are 0.

The rest of this ticket is the exact instruction for the model that does the work.

## 2. End goal
The component step of the installation wizard (`products/catalyst/bootstrap/ui/src/pages/wizard/steps/StepComponents.tsx`) lists, read-only, the eight rings of the kit (`docs/adr/0020`, S12) in their fixed order, each with its kit releases in install order, and the text "Every ring is installed. The service-model counts G3 and G9 of the wizard are 0.

## 3. The model (the law)
1. The rings and their order: `foundation`, `provider` (both required, never switched off), `observability`, `security-scanning`, `access`, `ai`, `commerce`, `operations`. A kit release is listed under the ring its label `catalyst.openova.io/ring` names (the catalogue's `bootstrapKit[].ring`), in install order.
2. A kit slot whose Blueprint is not in the catalogue, or whose name no chart under `platform/*/chart` or `products/*/chart` declares, is not listed. A slot with no ring or a ring outside the eight stops the generator (exit 2).
3. The wizard has no selection: the store's `selectedComponents` is every kit release (slug), whatever a persisted draft named, and the step renders no switch, checkbox or search box. Ring switches are not added here.
4. No hand list: no component table, no product family, no per-id logo or tone table. A component is a catalogue Blueprint; its name and summary are the card's, its ring is the kit's, its logo is the file of `public/component-logos` named after its slug, its dependencies are the kit's `dependsOn` and the card's `depends`.
5. New tooling is Go: the ring list is written by `tools/guards/cmd/kit-rings`, not by `scripts/generate-catalog.py`; no Python is added.
6. Deleted, and appended to `docs/ledger/deleted-paths.txt`: `componentGroups.ts`, `componentGroups.catalog-integrity.5575.test.ts`, `stepComponentsCopy.ts`, `logoTone.ts` (all under `products/catalyst/bootstrap/ui/src/pages/wizard/steps/`), `MarketplaceFamilyPage.tsx`, `MarketplaceProductPage.tsx`, `marketplaceCopy.ts` (under `products/catalyst/bootstrap/ui/src/pages/marketplace/`).
