# CMP-078 — Mutate policies to MutatingPolicy: app-label-mutate

## In plain words

**The problem today.** The policy that copies a workload's instance or name label into its application label is still written in the engine's legacy policy kind, which the engine line after the current one no longer serves. It is the last mutate policy of the policy chart in that kind, and it has no test suite of its own, so nothing shows that a rewrite keeps the label it adds and the objects it leaves alone.

**What changes.** The policy is written in the new mutating kind, in the same file and under the same name, and a new suite of eleven cases gives the same results and the same patched objects on the legacy policy and on the new one. The copy-is-current test keeps the suite's copy of the policy current, and the legacy count falls by one.

**Done means:**

- The policy chart renders app-label-mutate as a MutatingPolicy, and no template of the chart holds a legacy mutate rule.
- The policy matches Pods and the Deployment, StatefulSet, DaemonSet, ReplicaSet, ReplicationController, Job and CronJob kinds.
- The suite of the policy has eleven cases and passes with the same patched objects on the legacy policy and on the new one.
- A copy of the policy in the suite that is not the current render turns the pull request red.
- The chart's function test waits for the new policy to report ready.
- The legacy policy guard counts one object fewer: 23 on the base of this ticket.

The rest of this ticket is the exact instruction for the model that does the work.

## 3. The model (the law)
1. **In place.** The template keeps its path, its `metadata.name` `app-label-mutate`, its labels and annotations, its values keys `compliancePolicies.appLabelMutate.enabled` and `excludeNamespaces` and their defaults. The label it adds (`catalyst.openova.io/application`, with the value of `app.kubernetes.io/instance`, or of `app.kubernetes.io/name` when the instance label is absent or empty) and the objects it leaves alone do not change.
2. **Shape.** `apiVersion: policies.kyverno.io/v1`, `kind: MutatingPolicy`; `spec.evaluation.admission.enabled: true` and `spec.evaluation.background.enabled: false` (the legacy `background: false`); `spec.matchConstraints.resourceRules` with operations `CREATE` and `UPDATE` on `pods` and `replicationcontrollers` of the core group, `daemonsets`, `deployments`, `replicasets` and `statefulsets` of `apps`, `cronjobs` and `jobs` of `batch`, version `v1`; a variable `labels` that reads the labels of a Pod, of the Pod template of a CronJob's Job template, or of the Pod template of the other kinds (the variable of `stamp-cilium-enforced-label`, CMP-077); a variable `application`; three match conditions, `exclude-namespaces` (the chart helper `bp-kyverno-policies.excludeNamespacesExpr`), `application-label-absent` and `source-label-set`; one mutation, an `ApplyConfiguration` that sets the label at the path of the kind.
3. **Two rules become one.** The legacy rules `stamp-from-instance-label` (instance label set) and `stamp-from-name-label-fallback` (instance label empty, name label set) had preconditions that never both hold. The variable `application` makes the same choice: the instance value when it is not empty, else the name value, else the empty string, which `source-label-set` refuses.
4. **The kinds by name.** The legacy policy had two Pod rules, and the engine's autogen expanded them to the seven controller kinds. The new policy names the eight kinds itself, as CMP-077 does: the engine's autogen of a MutatingPolicy covers no ReplicationController and rewrites no null-safe label read.
5. **Same results.** The suite was green on the legacy render first (kyverno CLI v1.19.2), each result naming the legacy rule (`stamp-from-instance-label`, `stamp-from-name-label-fallback` or an autogen variant). Then only `policy.yaml` (the new render) and one key of each result (`rule:` became `isMutatingPolicy: true`) changed; the resources, the expected results and `patched.yaml` are the same files and give the same output. Case 3, an empty instance label, falls back to the name label on both kinds; case 6, an application label present with the empty value, is left alone on both.
6. **Current copy.** `tests/suites-current.sh` lists the suite in `SUITES` after `inject-gitea-source-secretref`; checks C2 and C4 already accept a MutatingPolicy (CMP-065).
7. **Baseline.** `scripts/baselines/kyverno-legacy.json` loses the entry of the template (24 to 23 objects on the base of this ticket).
8. **No legacy mutate rule left.** After this ticket no template of `platform/kyverno-policies/chart/templates` holds a line `mutate:`; on the base the two lines are this template's.
9. **One difference, by convention.** The legacy rules named no operation; the new policy matches `CREATE` and `UPDATE` only, as the policies rewritten by CMP-062 to CMP-065 and CMP-077 do.
